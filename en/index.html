MADE FOR EVERYDAY HORSE CARE

Less searching.
A more connected view.

The goal is one connected horse platform, built around a shared horse profile, with fewer duplicate entries and scattered tools. Training, feeding, health and daily care belong together. The current development build is a step towards that goal.

  • Keep separate records for multiple horses
  • Connect training, appointments and care
  • Follow records and changes over time

DEVELOPED WITH CARE

Your horse deserves
clear information.

01

Local at its core

The local app mode does not require an account. Optional cloud connectivity is prepared, but is not in production.

02

Data with a source

Feed values retain their source and data quality. Missing information is not presented as a certain result.

03

Records with clear limits

Hippovex supports organisation. It does not replace veterinary diagnosis or individual feeding advice.

ANY QUESTIONS?

Good to know.

Can I download Hippovex yet?+

Not yet. The app is in development. A public download and a confirmed release date are not available yet.

Will I need an account?+

The local app mode is implemented without an account. Optional cloud features are not released for production yet.

Which devices is Hippovex intended for?+

Android and iOS are intended platforms. An Android development build exists; real-device testing and iOS validation remain pending.

What will Hippovex cost?+

Pricing and the final feature scope have not been set. This website does not offer paid registration.

How can I give feedback?+

Use the email link in the contact section. It opens your own email app; you decide when to send your message.
